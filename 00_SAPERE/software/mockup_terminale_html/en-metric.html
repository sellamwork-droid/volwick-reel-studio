Metric: what to fix

Seven fixes to the metric selector and to how the walls are drawn when it changes.

22 Aug 2026For Matt
1

Separate ALL from the other three

ALL stays lit green even when it is off, and it sits wedged between CEX and DEX. Select GEX, or CEX, or DEX, and it stays green anyway: there is no way to tell whether multi-metric is on.

Put the three metrics in one block and ALL outside it, separated by a gap, in the same position it holds today. Two states only: off, with border and text dimmed; on, filled. When ALL is on none of the three stays selected, and clicking one of the three turns ALL off.

2

Weigh each metric against itself

The three arrive in different units: gamma in millions, charm in thousands, delta in billions. If line thickness is computed against a shared maximum, in multi-metric every delta wall becomes a solid band and the charm ones vanish — because of the unit, not because of how much they matter.

Compute the maximum separately for each metric, over its own visible walls. The strongest of each should carry the same visual weight as the strongest of the other two.

3

Draw fewer walls per metric in multi-metric

The wall count applies per metric, not in total: with Top 5 and all three on you get fifteen lines and fifteen labels, which pile up along the right edge.

Turning ALL on should drop the count by itself to the strongest of each metric. The value the user picked stays remembered and comes back when ALL goes off.

4

Tell the metrics apart by line style, not by colour

A wall's colour already says whether it brakes or accelerates: use it for the metric too and that information is lost.

Give each metric a line style. Gamma solid, charm long dashes, delta fine dots. The style scales with the thickness, so it stays recognisable on a strong wall and on a weak one alike.

It only applies in multi-metric. With a single metric every wall stays solid: there is nothing to tell apart. Colours never change.

5

Write the labels the same way every time

With a single metric the label reads SPX 7710, in multi-metric GEX·SPX 7710. The ticker appears twice on screen, since it is already in the window bar.

Drop the ticker and keep the order fixed: dot in the wall's colour, stars if it is flagged, level, value. In multi-metric the metric name goes in front of the level, and that is the only thing that changes.

6

Refit the view when the metric changes

The three metrics have their walls at different levels. Change metric and the scale stays where it was, the new walls end up off screen, and the chart sits empty without saying why.

After the switch, bring the view back onto the new metric's walls, or state how many are off screen and on which side.

7

Do not empty the chart during the switch

Change metric and the walls disappear, replaced for two or three seconds by «Loading dealer walls…».

Keep the old walls visible and dimmed until the new ones arrive, so the chart is never left bare. And do not re-request a metric already loaded in the same session: going back to one already seen should show it immediately.
